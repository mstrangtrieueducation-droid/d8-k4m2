UNIT 09 · THEORY

Closing Stores on Sundays

Buổi học số 25 · Học từ vựng, phân tích hai phía và chuẩn bị lập luận cho chủ đề tranh biện.

The debate question

Should stores close on Sundays? Closing can protect family time, rest and the environment. Staying open can improve convenience, jobs and income. A strong debater considers both sides before choosing a position.

Supporting

Workers get a regular day off; families have time together; businesses use less energy.

Opposing

Weekend shopping is convenient; part-time workers can earn money; small shops need sales.

Your task

Choose a side, give two clear reasons and answer one argument from the other side.
